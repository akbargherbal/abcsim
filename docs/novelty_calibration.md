## Chosen threshold

**containment >= 0.08** triggers the advisory. The maximum unrelated pair is
0.0199 (whole-bank unrelated max 0.0640, worst 0.0640) and the example song is not scored, so the threshold sits
above the observed false-positive floor while still catching almost every cluster pair.

## Limitations

- Exact 8-token n-grams are deliberately strict: a rhythmically varied variant
  can score low even when it is the same song. 2 counted cluster pair(s) are
  below the threshold for this reason; they are listed in
  `out/novelty_calibration.json`. Members with fewer than 8 tokens cannot be
  scored by this method and are not counted.
- The bank is vocal-only. Three kept songs have no usable Vocal stream and are
  not fingerprinted.
- The check is advisory, in the same spirit as CRAFT flags. It is not a
  plagiarism verdict and it does not prove originality.
- Runtime on a 30-bar score is ~0.3 s (well under 1 s).
